Code for estimated connected and disconnected reproduction numbers for UK and simulated data is in the folder `uk_spatial`.
`simulated_data` consists all the results related to simulated data.
`region_fitting` consists all the codes related to the case study: Regions of England and LTLAs of North East region.

Fig.2-3 : simulated_data/simulated_data_generation.R -> for generting simulated data
          simulated_data/simulated_fitting_region.R -> for estimating connected and disconnected reproduction numbers $R_t$ with simulated data 
          simulated_data/region_in_out_inf.R <- for the infections from three different sources

Fig.4 : region_fitting/fitting_region.R -> for estimating connected and disconnected $R_t$ over the 9 regions of England
Fig.5-7 : region_ftting/north_east_ltla_fitting.R -> for estimating connected and disconnected $R_t$ over the 12 twelve LTLAs of North East region of Engalnd.
          region_fitting/ltla_ne_in_out.R -> the infections from three different sources for the LTLAs of North East region of England
